Booking

How booking works with Alex

Send the shop request form or message me on Instagram. Tell me the idea, where it is going, and roughly how big. I draw every piece custom, so I will sketch it up and send it back for a look before we set a date.

A deposit holds your spot and comes off the price at your session. If you need to move a date, give me a couple of days of notice and we will find another one.
