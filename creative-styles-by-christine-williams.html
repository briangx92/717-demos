Meet Christine

Meet Christine

Christine Williams

Creative Styles by Christine Williams is a small salon at 1123 W Spruce St in Coal Township. The salon is owned by Christine Williams, whose name is part of the business name and its own business card reads 'Christine Williams, Owner' under the tagline 'A new look for the creative side of you!'.

The salon's interior features black chairs and dryer hoods on a wood-look flooring, creating a sophisticated and stylish environment. Christine brings her expertise in hair coloring, highlights, blowouts, and updos to help you achieve the perfect look for any occasion.
