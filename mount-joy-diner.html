Meet Mount Joy Diner

About the diner

Mount Joy Diner is a family restaurant in Mount Joy, PA, in the stone building with the red roof and the neon sign out front. It is a walk-in diner, with takeout for nights you would rather eat at home.

The menu is diner classics: two hot cakes, a Belgian waffle with blueberry topping, breakfast burritos, chicken fried steak, roast beef on stuffing, and the Mojo omelet, with home fries on the side.

Ordering

Walk-ins are welcome, but we also offer takeout and delivery. Place your order at the counter.
