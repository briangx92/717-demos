Scratch-baked, the way it’s always been done.

Services

Tap each one for the details. Everything is ordered by phone, please call the shop to place orders.

Cakes are special order only, in Banana, Banana Nut, Cherry, Cherry Nut, Chocolate, Marble, Orange Coconut, and Yellow, plus Pumpkin Roll in season.

Sizes: 9 inch Round · 1/4 Sheet (serves 20) · 1/2 Sheet (serves 40) · Full Sheet (serves 80).

Dessert trays and breakfast trays for your special occasions, in the shop’s own words, “excellent for showers, parties or the office,” and “sure to please.”

Alternate items are available by special request: just ask when you call.

Pies in Small (8 inch) and Large (10 inch): Apple, Cherry, Blueberry, Coconut Cream, Coconut Custard, Lemon Meringue, Cherry Berry, and Chocolate Cream, with Pumpkin and Strawberry in season, and a Four Berry Pie in a 6 inch size.

Fresh breads including the signature Irish soda breads, Cinnamon Bread, Challah, and Sandwich Buns. Cookies by the half pound or by the tray, and miniature desserts, creme puffs, eclairs, fruit tarts, and more.
