Boop's Barber Shop

Boop's Barber Shop is a full-service barber shop located at 314 Chestnut St in Mifflinburg, PA. The shop offers a range of services including haircuts, beard trims, hot towel shaves, and head shaves.

The interior features slate-blue walls and a wood-look plank floor, with a dark wood counter and a vintage barber chair. The brand is represented by the window lettering, with 'Boop's' in white script over 'BARBER SHOP' in white vintage block letters, and the phone number beneath.

Boop's is listed as a pickup spot for the Mifflinburg Free Press, indicating an active and open shop.
