Meet Boop's Barber Shop

Full-service barbering on Chestnut Street

Boop's Barber Shop is a full-service barber shop at 314 Chestnut St in Mifflinburg. Its own listing sums it up: 'Full service barber shop that includes haircuts, beard trims, etc.'

Inside: slate-blue walls, a wood-look plank floor, and a dark wood counter. The window carries the shop's name in white script and vintage block letters.

Boop's takes bookings online through Square and by phone, and doubles as a pickup spot for the Mifflinburg Free Press.
