Meet The Bagelsmith

Fresh bagels, full breakfast and lunch

The Bagelsmith is a bagel shop and breakfast and lunch restaurant on Butler Street in Easton, PA. In the shop's own words: 'A variety of flavored bagels delivered fresh daily. Serving a full breakfast and lunch menu.'

Breakfast runs to omelettes, pancakes, breakfast sandwiches, and home fries; lunch brings burgers and soup. The California Cheeseburger is served daily with pickle and chips for $6.50.

Dine in or take out. The kitchen closes 30 minutes before closing, so get orders in ahead of the rush.
