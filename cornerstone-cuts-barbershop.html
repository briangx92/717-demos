Cornerstone Cuts Barbershop

Cornerstone Cuts Barbershop is a traditional barbershop at 601 Chestnut St in Kulpmont, PA. The work is classic cuts, buzz cuts, and hot-towel straight-razor shaves in a clean, old-time shop.

Reviewers praise barber Brandon for unhurried, listen-first cuts. Inside: a vintage black-and-chrome barber chair, a red, white, and blue barber pole by the window, wood bench seating, and the shop dog holding down the floor.
