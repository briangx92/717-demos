Meet Brandon

Classic cuts, old-time charm

Brandon

Cornerstone Cuts Barbershop is a traditional barbershop in Kulpmont, PA. The shop offers classic cuts, buzz cuts, and hot-towel straight-razor shaves in a clean, vintage setting.

Barber Brandon is known for his unhurried, listen-first approach. Reviewers repeatedly praise his work, and the shop is also home to a friendly dog. The interior features a vintage black-and-chrome barber chair, a red/white/blue barber pole, and wood bench seating.
