The barber

Cutting since he was nine

Eric W., owner-barber

Eric started cutting hair at age nine. He graduated barber school in 2010 and founded Celebrity Barber Shop the same year, and he has been on Division St ever since.

The reviews tell the rest: clients write about coming back to the same chair for 10, 14, even 25 years. That is not an ad. That is a habit a lot of Harrisburg has.
