Meet Idol's Nails

Where beauty meets luxury

Idol's Nails is a full-service nail salon and spa on West Main Street in Lansdale. The menu runs from UV gel nails and Shellac gel manicures to waxing, facials, eyelash extensions, and tinting.

The salon hosts parties for weddings and birthdays, with refreshments for groups of six or more, and publishes a second number for texts. Inside: lavender-purple walls and a full wall of polish shelves.
