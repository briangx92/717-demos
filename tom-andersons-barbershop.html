Tom Anderson's Barbershop

Tom Anderson's Barbershop is a barbershop on N Hermitage Rd in Hermitage, PA. The shop is known for its patriotic-themed interior, featuring a barber chair draped with an American flag and a flag star on the wall.

The cash-only policy is clearly indicated on the storefront door, and appointments can be made by calling (724) 308-7114. The shop offers haircuts for $18 and beard trims for $5, as seen on the shop's own in-store letterboard.
