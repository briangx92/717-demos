About

Your neighborhood bar & cafe

Uncle Dre’s Cafe is a bar and cafe on Vernon Street in Harrisburg, offering dine-in and takeaway food alongside beer and cocktails.

The menu leans into the good stuff: seafood boils, wings, burgers and cheesesteaks, and soul food dinners — the kind of plates that bring the neighborhood back.

And when the kitchen winds down, the night keeps going: Uncle Dre’s doubles as a sports bar and pub, with live entertainment on the calendar.
