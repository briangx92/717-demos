Meet Waite Station Nails & Spa

A Boutique Salon by the Train Station

Waite Station Nails & Spa is a modern, all-natural nail salon by the Souderton train station, in its own words. The listing highlights EMCHI powder dip manicures, gel manicures, and pedicures.

Inside it runs warm beige with grey-blue trim and Edison-bulb pendant lights. Reservations are accepted and walk-ins are welcome.
