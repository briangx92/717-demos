Amy's Nail & Spa

Amy's Nail & Spa is a nail salon and spa located at 1142 W Elm Ave in Hanover, PA. The salon offers a range of services including pedicures, gel polish, manicures, facial waxing, beach nails, and nail enhancements.

The salon's work is vivid and color-forward, featuring deep red and black marble gel, hot pink with white floral art, purple holographic, and grey with glitter accents.
