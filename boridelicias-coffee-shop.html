Meet BoriDelicias Café

Authentic Puerto Rican flavor

BoriDelicias is a Puerto Rican coffee shop and dessert shop at 166 S Main St in Chambersburg, on Main Street for 4 years. Its logo says it plainly: 'Authentic Puerto Rican Flavor.'

The case holds flan, tres leches, tembleque, arroz con dulce, quesitos, and pastelillos de guayaba, and the coffee is Puerto Rican, from espresso at $2.50 to frappes.

Walk in for coffee and dessert, order delivery on DoorDash, or pre-order desserts by phone, 3 days to a week ahead depending on quantity.
