Meet BoriDelicias Coffee Shop

Discover a taste of Puerto Rico

BoriDelicias is the only Boricua coffee shop in the area. Located at 166 S Main St in downtown Chambersburg, PA, we offer a variety of authentic Puerto Rican treats including Puerto Rican coffee, quesitos, flan, tres leches cake, empanadas, and more.

Our desserts vary by day, so be sure to check our daily specials. We also offer DoorDash delivery and accept dessert/pastry pre-orders by phone, 3 days to 1 week ahead depending on quantity.
