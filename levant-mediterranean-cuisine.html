Our Story

A family table, opened to York

Levant is run by the Shalabi family, who opened in the summer of 2024 in the former Aviano’s space on South Duke Street — painting the exterior a vibrant mahogany red as part of the fit-out. In the summer of 2026, the restaurant marked two years in the Royal Square District.

The menu centers on the traditional dishes of Palestine and the Levant, cooked fresh — much of it in the wood-fire oven you can see from the dining room. Even the logo is family work: it was designed by the family’s daughter, Layan.
