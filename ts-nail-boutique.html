T's Nail Boutique

T's Nail Boutique is a nail salon in Tioga West Plaza, Tunkhannock, PA. Its own listing description is 'Professional Nail Care & Waxing,' and the published services run from nail art and designs to gel manicures, acrylics and pedicures.
