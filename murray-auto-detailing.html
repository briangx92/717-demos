Services

Three words, straight off their page

Murray describes the work in exactly three words, and publishes no packages and no prices, so none were invented for this page. Tap one open for what it covers.

01

Cleaning

The full wash and clean, inside and out, at your address.

The gallery below catches this work mid-stream: a Suburban under soap in a customer's driveway, hoses and cords still on the ground around a finished Ram. That is the model, the detail happens where the vehicle already sits, no drop-off, no waiting room.

02

Restoring

Bringing a tired vehicle back, not just washing it.

Restoring is the business's own word for the second half of the job: the vehicles in the finished shots (a G63 on a wooded lane, an Accord coupe gleaming on wet asphalt) are the after pictures. What a given vehicle needs is scoped on the call, since Murray publishes no set package list.

03

Protection

The finish that keeps the work from washing away.

Protection is the third of the business's own three words, the point of a detail that outlasts the next rainstorm. Which protection suits your vehicle is part of the booking conversation; no products or prices are promised here because the business publishes none.

04

RVs, Too

Fifth-wheels and big rigs, cleaned on their own pads.

Two of the six jobs in the gallery are the same KZ Venom fifth-wheel, mid-wash and finished on its gravel pad, ladder and long-handled brush in frame. If it fits in a driveway or on a pad, it is in scope.
