Clipper Jim's Barber Shop

Clipper Jim's Barber Shop is a traditional barber shop located on Golden Mile Rd in Towanda, PA. The shop offers a range of services including hot towel shaves, precision haircuts, beard trims, and makeovers.

The shop is open from Tuesday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and on Saturdays from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Closed on Mondays and Sundays.

Whether you're looking for a classic cut or a full makeover, Clipper Jim's Barber Shop has you covered.
