The shop

The Men's Room is a barber shop at 128 College Ave in Greenville, PA. It runs by appointment: call ahead, take your chair, pay cash. The menu covers the full range — fades and scissor cuts, long hair taken down to a clean finish, kids' cuts, beard work, and straight razor shaves.
