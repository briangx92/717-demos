The Men's Room

The Men's Room is a barber shop on College Avenue in Greenville, PA, near Thiel College. The shop opened in 2009 and offers men's haircuts and styling for all ages, with a hot shave around the ears and neck included with the service.

The shop operates by appointment and accepts cash only. Call to schedule your visit.
