About the Shop

Part coffee shop, part gallery

The Tiger Eye is a cozy coffee shop on Derry St in Harrisburg that also serves ice cream and baked goods. The shop doubles as a gallery and marketplace for local art and handmade items, so the mug in your hand and the piece on the wall may both be made by someone nearby.

Grab a table inside, or take one of the colorful seats out front. It is the kind of place built for lingering over a second cup.
