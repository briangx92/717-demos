Envy Nails & Spa

Envy Nails & Spa is a nail salon & spa in Logan Towne Center, Altoona, PA. We offer a range of services from Manicure, Pedicure to Nail Extensions, always striving to deliver the highest level of customer satisfaction.

Our warm cream/beige interior features wood manicure tables, pedicure spa chairs, and a full polish wall, creating a relaxing environment for your nail care needs.
