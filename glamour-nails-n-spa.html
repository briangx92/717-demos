Meet Glamour Nails-N-Spa

Professional Nails Salon

Glamour Nails-N-Spa

Glamour Nails-N-Spa is a professional nail salon located on Main Street in Greenville, PA. The salon is known for its fun, friendly staff who prioritize customer satisfaction and maintain good work ethics.

The salon offers a range of services including SNS dip nails, LCN gel nails, acrylic nails, pink & white nails, manicures, pedicures, and nail enhancements. The staff is led by experienced technicians named Kevin, Jose, Mimi, and Diana.

Facials and waxing services are also available, making Glamour Nails-N-Spa a one-stop destination for all your beauty needs.
