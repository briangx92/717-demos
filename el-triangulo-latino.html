Meet El Triangulo Latino

A lively Dominican haven

El Triangulo Latino is a family-owned Dominican restaurant on Manheim Ave in Lancaster, PA. Its own description reads, 'El Triangulo Latino is a family owned Dominican Restaurant. We serve fresh made-to-order food!!'

The restaurant's lively, neon-lit dining room plays Dominican music and features a kids menu. The logo, a red triangle with a chef-hat cloche, gold laurel, and gold script lettering on a deep navy blue field, stands out with the tagline 'Rico y Sabroso' and 'Since 2013'.

The menu includes a variety of dishes like mofongo, pernil, and lomo saltado. For those seeking a quick bite, options like the ham and cheese sandwich and chicken sandwich are available. Kids can enjoy cheese fries and chicken nuggets and fries.
