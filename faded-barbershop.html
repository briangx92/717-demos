Faded Barbershop

Faded Barbershop is a barbershop in Harrisburg, PA, also operating as Faded Precision Barbering. The shop is known for its clean, friendly environment and offers a range of services including haircuts, fades, and beard work for both adults and kids.

Reviewers point to the quality of the work and the friendly atmosphere. The shop runs appointments through Booksy and takes walk-ins, and it is open seven days a week.
