Faded Barbershop

Faded Barbershop is a clean, friendly barbershop in Harrisburg, PA. Located at 6110 Derry St, the shop offers haircuts, fades, beard trims, and straight razor shaves for both adults and kids.

Customer reviews highlight the shop's attention to detail and friendly atmosphere.

Faded also operates as Faded Precision Barbering. Same chairs and the same standard under either name.
