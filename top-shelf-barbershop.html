Meet Top Shelf Barber Co

Top Shelf Barber Co

Top Shelf Barber Co is a barber shop located at 246 Queen St in Northumberland, PA. Known for its one-on-one service, the shop offers a variety of haircut options, including a kids haircut and a haircut with a hot towel shave.

Every visit is one-on-one and booked ahead — haircuts, haircut and beard, kids haircuts, and haircuts with a hot towel shave.
