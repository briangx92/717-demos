Meet The Masters

Classic cuts in a historic barbershop

The Masters is a classic barbershop on Mill Street in downtown Danville, PA. Customer reviews describe traditional walk-in service with short waits.

The shop front is the brand: a deep burgundy-red awning with 'the MASTERS' in white serif capitals over red brick, and a classic red, white, and blue barber pole at the door. Inside: haircuts, beard trims, and shaves.
