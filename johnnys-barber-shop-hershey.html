Down Under

Old-style shop, new-style feel

The owner is John Christopher, identified as this shop's owner by national publications: CVS's beard-care guide names him a past president of the National Barber Boards Association and the owner of Johnny's Barbershop Downunder in Hershey, and a 2007 Hershey news story found him in the shop after 24 years working at the Hershey chocolate plant.

"Our shop is an old-style barbershop with a new style feel. It is a combination of nostalgic and state of the art."John C. Christopher, quoted in Sea Island magazine's barbershop feature

The room says the same: the vintage chair and boxing walls on one side, modern stations and tool chests on the other. Nostalgic and state of the art, on Chocolate Ave.
