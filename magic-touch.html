Magic Touch

Magic Touch is a family-friendly beauty salon in downtown Mansfield, PA, with over 50 years of service. It is known for hair color, particularly chunky lowlights, and for hair extensions.

The strongest published evidence is the nail work: cat-eye polish, tortoise shell nails, ombre and white French sets, all in the gallery below.
