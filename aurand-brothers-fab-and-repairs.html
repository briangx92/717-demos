Aurand Brothers Fab & Repairs

Aurand Brothers Fab & Repairs is a family-owned auto repair and fabrication shop located on Route 522 in Middleburg, PA. We specialize in state inspections, fabrication, and both large and small repairs. Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction is reflected in every job we take on.

Our shop is equipped with the latest tools and technology to ensure your vehicle is repaired to the highest standards. Whether you need a routine check-up or a major repair, our team is here to get the job done right. Visit us for reliable and efficient auto services.

Open Mon – Fri

We are open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed on weekends.
