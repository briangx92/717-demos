Meet Schmeck's Villa

Welcome to Schmeck's Villa

Schmeck's Villa is an Italian and American restaurant located at 4581 Boyertown Pike in Reading, PA. Our menu features a variety of delicious dishes, including escargots, Italian breaded calamari rings, and more. We offer a warm and inviting atmosphere, perfect for a relaxing dining experience.

Our daily and weekly food specials make it easy to find something that suits your taste. Whether you're in the mood for crispy hot wings, garlic bread, or a pulled pork slider, we've got you covered.

In the restaurant's own words: great food and service in an inviting and relaxed atmosphere. Check the Facebook page for daily and weekly specials.
