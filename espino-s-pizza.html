Espino's Pizza

Espino's Pizza is a small mom-and-pop shop on W Lemon St in Lancaster, PA. It's known for its NY-style pizza and Puerto Rican / Latin dishes. The shop is a favorite among local pizza enthusiasts, often praised as the best mom-and-pop pick in a pizza crawl.

Delivery and take-out options are available, and you can call to place your order. The shop does not accept reservations, so feel free to drop by for a quick slice or grab some to go.
