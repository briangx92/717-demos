Meet Bud Godek Auto Body

A full-service body shop

Bud Godek Auto Body

Bud Godek Auto Body is a full-service auto body shop on Fire Stone Ln in Middleburg, PA. The work is complete body work, collision repair, painting and frame straightening, done in the red shop building on Fire Stone Lane.

The shop is also a longtime sponsor of the Ken Duke Jr. #67 RaceSaver sprint car out of Selinsgrove. The classics in the gallery, a white C1 Corvette and a purple Fox-body Mustang, came through this shop.
