Head Hunter Barbershop

Head Hunter Barber Shop is a barber shop on E Wellsboro St in Mansfield, PA. Behind the chair: 7 years of experience and a focus on clean, consistent cuts. The shop's interior features deep green walls, a black tin ceiling, warm wood floors and cabinetry, with a white pedestal sink and black barber chairs.

A skeleton-barber motif recurs in the shop's own wall art and signage, and the classic red/white/blue barber pole stands at the storefront. Every cut is held to one standard: yours.
