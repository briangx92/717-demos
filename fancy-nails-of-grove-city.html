Fancy Nails of Grove City

Fancy Nails of Grove City is a nail salon in the Pine Grove Village area of Grove City, PA. The menu covers dip powder, gel and acrylic work, nail extensions and nail art.

Manicures and pedicures include men's services. The sets in the gallery are the salon's own work, heavy on ombre fades and metallic finishes. Call (724) 450-0342 to book.

Dip, gel & acrylic

Dip powder, gel extensions and acrylic nails, plus nail art and extensions.

Men's services too

Men's manicures and men's pedicures are on the menu alongside the regular manicure and pedicure.
