A&R Nails

A&R Nails is an appointment-only nail salon in Coal Township, in the Shamokin area. Services are offered as timed nail-service sessions ranging from 30 minutes to 2 hours, including manicures and pedicures.

Each session is customized to your needs, with detailed nail art that ranges from simple manicures to intricate designs. The salon's work is presented against a pink crystal prop on a silver glitter backdrop, showcasing the quality and creativity of each set.

Book your appointment online at arnails70.booksy.com/a/ or call/text (570) 205-1458 to schedule your visit.
