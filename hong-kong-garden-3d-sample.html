OUR STORY

The oldest Chinese restaurant in Lancaster County.

Hong Kong Garden opened its doors in 1980, same location, same business name, same family ever since. Families have been bringing their second and third generations through our doors for years.

Fresh, quality Chinese cuisine at reasonable prices, generous portions, and a relaxed 120-seat dining room. Voted the county's #1 Chinese restaurant by Lancaster County Magazine nineteen years running.
