The old-school barbershop

Jess Hadfield

Jess Hadfield's is a traditional barbershop in Mount Carmel, PA, owned by Jess Hadfield. The published menu is simple: haircuts, beard trims and shaves.

Inside: cream walls and warm wood browns, a pressed-tin ceiling, deep-red vinyl waiting seating, and a wall covered in patches and insignia.
