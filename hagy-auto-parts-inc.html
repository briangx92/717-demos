Hagy's Auto Parts

Hagy's Auto Parts in Ephrata is an auto repair shop and parts store on East Main Street. The tall road sign out front lists the work plainly: auto repair, an auto parts store, and towing.

The sign also carries the official plaques of a PA emissions and inspection station, so state and emissions inspections happen here too. Repair, parts, inspections, and towing, all from the same shop.
