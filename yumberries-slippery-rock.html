Meet Yumberries Slippery Rock

Your Swirl On!

Yumberries Slippery Rock is a self-serve frozen yogurt and dessert shop located on S Main St in downtown Slippery Rock, PA, next to Slippery Rock University. We offer 12 flavors of the freshest and healthiest frozen yogurt with certified live cultures in every bite!

With over 40 toppings, there is something to make everyone smile. Grab a cup, top it, weigh, pay... YUM!! It's perfect every time because you make it the way you like it.
