Meet Flash Nails

Flash Nails

Flash Nails is a nail salon on Carlisle Pike in Mechanicsburg, PA. The shop is known for its variety of nail services including acrylics, manicures, pedicures, gel and dip nails, and waxing.

The salon is also active on social media, particularly on Facebook, where they frequently share photos of finished nail sets. This includes seasonal and holiday designs, such as 4th of July, Easter, and Valentine's Day themes.
