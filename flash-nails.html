Flash Nails

Flash Nails is a family-run nail salon located on Carlisle Pike in Mechanicsburg, PA. The salon offers a range of services including acrylics, gel nails, and waxing. They are known for their seasonal and holiday nail sets, which they frequently post on Facebook.

The salon posts its finished sets to Facebook often, especially seasonal and holiday work. Walk-ins are welcome, and appointments are by phone at (717) 909-8080.
