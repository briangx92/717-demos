Meet Diamond Nails & Hair

A walk-in nail salon with many services

Diamond Nails & Hair

Diamond Nails & Hair is a walk-in nail salon on Main Street in Greenville, PA. The menu runs deep: classic and gel manicures, hot stone manicures, acrylic and ombré full sets, and four tiers of pedicure.

The work the salon and its customers post leans jewel tones: red glitter, purple, cobalt, and coral. Walk-ins are the house rule, and the chairs cover hands and feet.
