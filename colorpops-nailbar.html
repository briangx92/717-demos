Meet ColorPops Nailbar

Where nails become works of art

ColorPops Nailbar is a nail salon at 743 W Main St in Lansdale, PA. Their own description says it: 'Where nails become works of art and colors come alive.'

The menu runs to manicures and pedicures, dip powder nails, gel nail extensions, acrylic nails and nail art, plus men's manicures and foot massages.

Prices are posted in the salon instead of hidden: pedicure tiers run from a $20 Basic to a $55 Royal on the chalkboard, and the sidewalk board lists a Classic Mani at $15, a Gel Mani at $30 and Powder Dip at $40.
