Meet Duane

A barber with a first-class touch

Duane DeWire

First Class Male is a barber shop at 103 S Market St in Selinsgrove, PA. The name is a play on words, directly across from the Selinsgrove Post Office, with an oversized first-class envelope on a barber-pole post outside the shop.

Owner Duane DeWire has over 40 years of experience and is known for affordable haircuts and expert beard trims. The shop is a red-brick building with a white-columned portico, offering a classic yet welcoming atmosphere.
