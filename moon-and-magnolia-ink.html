Meet Moon & Magnolia Ink

Moon & Magnolia Ink

Moon & Magnolia Ink is a tattoo shop at 335 Stonycreek St in Johnstown, PA. The shop's own listing description says it works by appointment Wednesday through Sunday, and is closed Monday and Tuesday.

The shop publishes custom tattoo work and runs themed flash days, including a Veteran's Flash event with a military appreciation discount. Book through Facebook Messenger, by phone, or through the artist's Instagram, @tiffnicoletattoos.
