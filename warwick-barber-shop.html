Meet Warwick Barber Shop

Warwick Barber Shop

Warwick Barber Shop is a classic Americana barber shop established in 2016 on Lititz Pike in Lititz, PA. The shop offers a traditional, family-shop feel with chrome, navy, and the red-white-and-blue pole.

The services are the barber shop staples: haircuts for men, women, and kids, beard trims, and straight razor head shaves.

Inside, a distressed flag covers one wall and the pole spins out front. Call ahead to book your chair.
