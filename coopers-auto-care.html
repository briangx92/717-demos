Cooper's Auto Care

Cooper's Auto Care is a family-owned and operated auto repair shop in Hermitage. The shop has recently relocated to a more centralized location at 2050 Highland Rd.

The building sign proudly displays the shop's commitment to all makes and models, offering a range of services including oil changes, tune-ups, engine swaps, transmissions, brakes, tires, and a state inspection station.
