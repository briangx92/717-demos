Meet The American Diner

A classic Penn Avenue diner

The American Diner

The American Diner is an all-day diner in West Reading, PA. Its own description announces: 'We are OPEN for Dining in,' with the full menu available for takeout and curbside pickup, and delivery through its GrubHub partnership.

The menu names the classics: burgers, pancakes, surf and turf, a French dip, club sandwiches, cheesesteaks, French onion soup, mac & cheese, and a banana split or an eclair to finish. The diner thanks service members year-round with a Service Members Discount.

Breakfast all day

The diner's own description leads with its breakfast all-day menu, and a senior citizen menu is available.
