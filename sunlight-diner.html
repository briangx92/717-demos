Meet Sunlight Diner

Family-friendly 1950s-style diner

Sunlight Diner is a family-friendly 1950s-style diner located on Gettysburg Road in Mechanicsburg, PA. The diner has a unique ambiance with Elvis and Marilyn Monroe wall art and a black-and-white checkered floor.

The menu offers a variety of classic diner dishes such as waffles with ham, hot oatmeal with milk, golden brown pancakes, and waffles with bacon. Additionally, they serve chicken pot pie, broiled seafood platter, cinnamon toast, crab cakes, dumpling soup, and corned beef hash. The diner also provides catering services.

Sunlight Diner is open from 5:30 AM to 8:30 PM on weekdays, 6:00 AM to 8:30 PM on Saturdays, and 7:00 AM to 8:30 PM on Sundays. Breakfast is served all day, and the diner offers both takeout and delivery options.
