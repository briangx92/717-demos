Susan's Beauty Parlor: Professional, Reliable, Affordable

Susan's Beauty Parlor

Susan's Beauty Parlor is a local beauty salon located on Main Street in Greenville. The salon is known for its professional, reliable, and affordable services.

Services include haircuts, styling, and coloring, as well as manicures, pedicures, facials, massage therapy, and permanent makeup. For tanning, appointments are scheduled by phone in the afternoon.
