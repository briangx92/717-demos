Susan's Beauty Parlor

Susan's Beauty Parlor is a local salon on Main Street in Greenville. The parlor is known for its professional and reliable services, making it a go-to spot for those seeking affordable beauty treatments.

The salon offers a range of services from haircuts and styling to manicures and pedicures. For those looking to relax, facials and massage therapy are also available. Permanent makeup and tanning services are provided for a more permanent touch.

Afternoon appointments are scheduled by phone, ensuring that each client receives the attention they deserve.
