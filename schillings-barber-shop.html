Meet Helmut

A barber shop with tradition

Helmut Schilling

Schilling's Barber Shop is an old-school barber shop in Souderton, PA, established in 1925. Owner Helmut Schilling bought the shop in 1977 and runs it as a father-son team.

The menu is cuts, a hot towel shave, beard trims, and head shaves. Walk-ins are welcome, and the shop is also bookable through Fresha.
