Schilling's Barber Shop

Schilling's Barber Shop is a barber shop in Souderton, PA, established in 1925. Published descriptions say Helmut Schilling bought the shop in 1977 and runs it as a father-son team; he is the owner and lead barber.

The list is classic: haircuts, hot towel shaves, beard trims and head shaves. Walk-ins are welcome; bookings also run through Fresha.
