Meet Hull's

Cutting and styling since 1969

Hull's is a barber shop located at 600 Baltimore St in Hanover, PA. The business has been serving clients since 1969, offering a range of hair cutting and styling services.

The shop is known for its classic and vintage atmosphere, with a logo that features a round sign with 'HULL'S BARBER SHOP' in black slab lettering on aged cream, flanked by two barber poles, a straight razor, and scissors below, all enclosed in a deep-red circular border.

Open Hours

Open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday from 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Closed on Wednesday and Sunday.
