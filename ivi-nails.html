Chapter one: the work

Painted by hand, one nail at a time

Hand-painted nail art is on the salon's own service list, and it shows in the work it posts. On this set the art is mixed in, not spread across every finger: green on most nails, a French tip in the same green, and one gold glitter nail carrying a small painted bow.

If you have a design in mind, say so in your request below and bring it up when the salon gets back to you.
