WHERE THIS “INVENTION” REALLY CAME FROM

  • Warm paper background, huge whitespace, thin horizon line, breathing sun — this isn’t invented. It’s the wellness-app playbook (Calm, Headspace) crossed with an Apple keynote slide. Faced with no category pattern, I reached for the nearest shelf.
  • Soft euphemistic copy (“begin quietly,” “lighter”) — the hospice-brochure register. Tasteful, but borrowed.
  • Notice what’s missing: no new layout idea, no novel interaction, no visual metaphor that didn’t already exist. I changed the wardrobe, not the body.

The limit, stated plainly.

I have excellent shelves — deep, organized, well-lit. I can walk to the right one in seconds and arrange its contents beautifully. What I can’t do is build a new shelf: a visual language with no precedent that still feels inevitable. When there’s no pattern, I decorate — I don’t discover.

For your business, that’s the right tool: every prospect you pitch has a category, and I know every shelf.
