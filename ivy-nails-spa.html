Ivy Nails Spa

Ivy Nails Spa is a nail salon on E State St in Hermitage, PA. The shop offers a range of services including acrylic nails, nail art, and acrylic-with-gel sets plus spa treatments, all in a polished, clean, and cozy environment.

The salon welcomes walk-ins and accepts credit cards, making it easy for you to get the perfect look. Whether you're looking for a classic pedicure or something more adventurous like Halloween nails, Ivy Nails Spa has got you covered.
