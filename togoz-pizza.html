Meet John

Tow-goes, since 2013

John

Togoz Pizza is a small take-out and delivery pizza shop in Wyoming borough, PA. Opened October 7, 2013 by owner John (first name as published by NEPA Pizza Review), Togoz blends a pan-fried Sicilian crust with Old Forge-style sauce and cheese blends. The name is pronounced 'tow-goes.'

The named pies: the Valley pizza with raw shredded mozzarella, white pizza, fried pizza, white tomato garlic and mac and cheese pizza, plus cheesesteaks, boneless bites, cut pizza and miniature pierogies.
