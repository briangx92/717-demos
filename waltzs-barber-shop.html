Barber Shop · Mechanicsburg, PA

Traditional barbering, kept the old way.

Waltz's Barber Shop is a traditional barber shop on Williams Grove Road in Mechanicsburg. Haircuts, hot towel shaves, beard trims and head shaves, done the way they have always been done: a real conversation, a careful cut, and a hot towel finish.

The shop holds a 4.9 star rating across 21 Google reviews. No website until now, no online booking, just a phone number and a chair. This page gives the shop a home online: services, hours, and a simple way to ask for an appointment.

Inside the Shop

Paper cards, a ledger, and a red tumbler.

Appointments at Waltz's still run on paper cards and a ledger on the counter. The shop's red tumbler sits next to them. It is that kind of place, and regulars would not have it any other way.

The request form on this page keeps that spirit: you write down what you want, and the shop confirms with you directly. Nothing is booked until you hear back.
