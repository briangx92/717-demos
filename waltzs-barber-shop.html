Inside

At the counter

Paper cards, a ledger, and the shop's own tumbler

The counter at Waltz's still runs the way barbershops always have: a box of paper appointment cards, a ledger sheet, and the shop's scissors logo etched on a red tumbler. Nothing about it is in a hurry, and that is the point.

It is also why the phone is the front door here. Call ahead, get your name in the book, and take your chair when you arrive.
