Poppteas

Poppteas is a bubble tea shop and cafe in Allentown, PA, also listed as a smoothie & juice bar and snack bar. Review summaries describe very friendly, quick service.

Drinks named by customers include taro with brown sugar jelly, vanilla milk tea with coffee boba and masala chai with coffee boba. On the snack side: savory and sweet popcorns and chicken popcorns.
