Meet Cinderella Nail and Tanning

Making Life Beautiful, One Nails Set at a Time

Cinderella Nail and Tanning

Cinderella Nail and Tanning is a nail and tanning salon in the Sunnybrook Shopping Center in Pottstown, PA. Their own line: 'Making Life Beautiful, One Nails Set at a Time.'

Inside it is bright cream and warm wood floors, brass lamps and blush accents, with a big geometric horse tapestry on the wall that you will not mistake for any other salon.

The menu covers manicures and pedicures, dip powder, gel and acrylic work, nail art, and tanning beds in the same visit.

Walk-ins & booking

Walk-ins are accepted, and the salon also takes bookings through the Maby app. Booking ahead is the safer bet, especially near closing.
