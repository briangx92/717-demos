B & B Services Center

B & B Services Center is a sole-proprietor auto repair shop established in 2001. Located at 201 E 5th St in Mount Carmel, PA, the shop offers general auto repair and state inspections. With a staff of about two, the business is known for its hands-on approach and reliable service.

The shop is open from Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. No itemized service menu, prices, or booking policy is published, but customers have reported satisfaction with the state inspections done there.
