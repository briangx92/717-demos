What the shop does

The core of the business — general auto repair out of the bays on Buford Ave. Call with what's going on and the shop takes it from there.

A registered PA state inspection station — inspections are on the shop's own list of services. Due for yours? Call and get on the books.

New tires and a working stock of used ones — the trailer out back is stacked with them. Tire repair too: one customer's photo shows four screws pulled out of her tire, plugged at this shop.

The maintenance staples — oil changes and tune ups, straight off the shop's own service list.

Headed out on the road? A trip check before you go beats a breakdown two states away.

The shop sells used cars, too — the TOM KNOX USED CARS sign has stood behind a lot of new owners' first photos with their cars.
