Our Story

An old gas station, a Palmyra staple.

The Filling Station has been family-owned and operated for over 25 years — and yes, the building really did start life as a gas station. These days the pumps are gone, but the idea is the same: pull in, fuel up, head out happy.

Breakfast starts early, the coffee keeps coming, and the donut case is filled fresh daily — it's the first thing most regulars check when they walk in. Lunch and dinner plates round out the day, and takeout is always an option when the kitchen at home feels like too much work.

No reservations, no dress code, no fuss. Just a hometown diner doing what it has done for a quarter century.
