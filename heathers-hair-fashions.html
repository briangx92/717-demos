Heather's Hair Fashions

Heather's Hair Fashions is a small hair studio located on Hillcrest Ave in Souderton, PA. Known for her expertise in color and highlights, Heather runs the studio with a one-client-at-a-time approach.

With over 34 years in the business, Heather brings a wealth of experience to each appointment. From balayage and keratin treatments to hair extensions and permanent straightening, she offers a range of services to suit every client's needs.

Whether you're looking for a new highlight or a perm, Heather's Hair Fashions is the place to go. Call to book your appointment and experience the difference of one-on-one care.
