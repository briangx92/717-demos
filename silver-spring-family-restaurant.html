The Agadis family's diner

Silver Spring Family Restaurant

Silver Spring Family Restaurant is a family diner in Lancaster, PA. In its own words it is 'famous for serving breakfast all day, including homemade buttermilk pancakes and outstanding traditional local fare.'

The Agadis family runs the diner and, in their words, looks forward to 'providing their customers with fast and friendly service, a great meal at a fair price in a warm setting.'

Breakfast all day

Breakfast is served all day, including the homemade buttermilk pancakes the diner is known for.
