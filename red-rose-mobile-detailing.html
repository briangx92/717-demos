GOOD TO KNOW

Booking policies, plain and simple.

Cancellation

  • 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule
  • Late cancellations: $25 fee
  • No-shows: 50% of service total

When we arrive

  • Vehicle ready, accessible, keys available
  • Can't start within 15 min: may cancel/reschedule with fee
  • Can't access the vehicle: treated as no-show

Weather

Extreme weather may delay or reschedule your appointment. We'll notify you as early as possible. Reschedules are always free.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to provide anything?
Just make sure the vehicle is accessible with keys available. We'll confirm any other setup details when you book.
What if it rains on my appointment day?
If weather prevents safe, quality work, we'll reschedule you at no cost and notify you as early as possible.
How do I pay?
Payment is due when the service is complete. We accept all major cards, cash, and mobile payments via Square.
How long does a detail take?
From 40 minutes for a Wash and Wipe to 5+ hours for ceramic coating. Each package above lists its typical time.
Do you offer gift options or boats?
Yes, we do full boat detailing (priced by footage, ask for a quote). Call us and we'll work it out.
