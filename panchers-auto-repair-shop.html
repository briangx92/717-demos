Pancher's Auto Repair Shop

Pancher's Auto Repair Shop is a locally owned auto body and mechanical shop on S Franklin St in Shamokin, PA. Their own listing puts it plainly: body and mechanical work, locally owned for over 45 years.

The shop works on all models, foreign and domestic: collision repair, mechanical repair and auto body work. It is also a PA Official Inspection Station, the blue-and-gold keystone by the bay door, so inspections and repairs happen in the same place.

Foreign & domestic

Mechanical and collision work on all models, foreign and domestic. That is the promise on the shop's own sign.
