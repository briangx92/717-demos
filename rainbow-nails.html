Rainbow Nails

Rainbow Nails is a nail salon located at 5817 Forbes Ave in Squirrel Hill, Pittsburgh, PA. The salon offers a range of services including spa pedicure and manicure, deluxe spa pedicure & manicure, gel polish, acrylic nails, and nail art.

The salon features a full-height illuminated polish wall and numbered white pedicure spa chairs, providing a relaxing and organized environment for your nail care needs.
