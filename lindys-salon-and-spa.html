Lindy's Salon and Spa

Lindy's Salon and Spa is an all-in-one salon and spa in Hermitage, PA: hair, nails, lashes, facials, massage and tanning under one roof, with a friendly licensed team.

The business formerly traded as Lindy's At The Beach and At The Beach Tanning Salon. Highlights and coloring, perms and Brazilian blowouts, waxing and permanent cosmetics are all on the menu.
