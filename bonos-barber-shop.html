Meet Bono's Barber Shop

Old-school, the way a barber shop should be

Bono's Barber Shop is an old-school barber shop on E Main Street in Norristown. In its own words, it 'offers quality haircuts at affordable prices for the whole family.'

The shop keeps a vintage feel inside: chrome hydraulic chairs, a tan patterned tile floor, and the classic barber pole in the window.

Bono's opens three days a week, Wednesday through Friday. Haircuts for adults and kids alike.
