Bono's Barber Shop

Bono's Barber Shop is a classic barber shop located on E Main St in Norristown. It offers quality haircuts at affordable prices for the whole family.

The shop is open three days a week, on Wednesdays, Thursdays, and Fridays, from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, with extended hours on Fridays until 4:00 PM.

The interior features a vintage look with tan patterned tile floors, chrome hydraulic barber chairs, and salmon-pink lower walls.
